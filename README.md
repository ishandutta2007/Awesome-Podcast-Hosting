## Top Podcast Hosting Ecosystem


**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Podcast Hosting, RSS Publishing, Podcast Websites, Analytics, Distribution, Monetization & Podcast Management*  
**Last updated: August 2026**


This repository tracks notable **SaaS/hosted platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Podcast Hosting**. These tools help podcasters upload and distribute audio/video episodes, generate and manage RSS feeds, create podcast websites and players, track audience analytics, manage subscribers, monetize shows, and distribute content to podcast directories.


**Examples** include Buzzsprout, Transistor, Captivate, Simplecast, Podbean, Spreaker, Castos, Libsyn, RedCircle, and RSS.com. Modern podcast-hosting platforms increasingly combine hosting with analytics, websites, monetization, dynamic content, video podcasting, transcription, audience engagement, and distribution to major podcast directories. Buzzsprout, for example, provides podcast hosting, distribution, websites, analytics, monetization, transcripts, and support for both audio and video podcasting. ([buzzsprout.com](https://www.buzzsprout.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))


**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with open-source podcast-hosting platforms, RSS publishing systems, podcast CMSs, podcast players, analytics infrastructure, Podcasting 2.0 tooling, self-hosted media stacks, and developer libraries. The open-source ecosystem is particularly interesting because podcasting itself is fundamentally based on open RSS distribution.


Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or repositories.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms


- **[Buzzsprout](https://www.buzzsprout.com/)**  
  Podcast hosting platform providing audio/video hosting, RSS distribution, podcast websites, analytics, transcripts, monetization, dynamic content, and podcast-directory distribution. ([buzzsprout.com](https://www.buzzsprout.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))


- **[Transistor](https://transistor.fm/)**  
  Professional podcast hosting platform supporting multiple podcasts, RSS feeds, analytics, podcast websites, private podcasts, subscribers, and team collaboration.


- **[Captivate](https://www.captivate.fm/)**  
  Podcast hosting and marketing platform focused on podcast publishing, analytics, websites, audience growth, marketing, subscriptions, and monetization.


- **[Simplecast](https://simplecast.com/)**  
  Podcast hosting and analytics platform providing publishing, RSS distribution, customizable websites and players, analytics, and audience-management tools.


- **[Podbean](https://www.podbean.com/)**  
  Podcast hosting and publishing platform offering podcast websites, mobile apps, analytics, live streaming, advertising, premium content, and monetization.


- **[Spreaker](https://www.spreaker.com/)**  
  Podcast hosting, live broadcasting, distribution, analytics, and monetization platform with tools for both independent and professional publishers.


- **[Castos](https://castos.com/)**  
  Podcast hosting platform providing publishing, analytics, podcast websites, private podcasts, transcription, YouTube republishing, and audience-growth features.


- **[Libsyn](https://libsyn.com/)**  
  Long-running podcast hosting and distribution platform providing media hosting, RSS feeds, analytics, advertising, monetization, websites, and podcast-network tools.


- **[RedCircle](https://redcircle.com/)**  
  Podcast hosting and monetization platform focused on independent creators, dynamic advertising, listener subscriptions, donations, cross-promotion, and analytics.


- **[RSS.com](https://rss.com/)**  
  Podcast hosting and distribution platform providing RSS publishing, analytics, podcast websites, monetization, podcasting networks, and Podcasting 2.0 capabilities.


- **[Transistor for Business](https://transistor.fm/)**  
  Podcast hosting infrastructure for companies, organizations, and teams managing multiple professional podcasts with analytics, websites, and distribution.


- **[Fireside](https://fireside.fm/)**  
  Podcast hosting platform providing podcast publishing, analytics, websites, media hosting, and customizable podcast experiences.
